The problem

Direct bill statements don't reconcile themselves

Every statement cycle turns into the same manual slog — and the two systems rarely speak the same language.

Line-by-line by hand

Comparing a PDF carrier statement against an AMS360 export one row at a time, every single cycle.

Policy numbers never quite match

Carrier IDs and AMS360 policy numbers are formatted differently, so simple lookups miss real matches.

Scanned statements, no clean data

Some carriers still send image-only PDFs that won't paste into a spreadsheet at all.

What it does

Built specifically for direct bill reconciliation

Not a spreadsheet macro — a matching engine that understands how carrier and agency management data actually differ.

Two-File Matching

Drop a carrier statement and an AMS360 export — PDF, Excel, or CSV — and the engine reconciles every policy automatically.

Smart Fallback Matching

Matches by policy number first, then by client name and dollar amount when the numbers don't line up — and flags every fallback match for a quick check.

Six-Way Status Detail

Match, Amount Mismatch, Policy # Differs, Verify Detail, Missing from AMS360, Missing from Carrier — never a vague "discrepancy" bucket.

Built-In OCR

Reads scanned, image-only carrier PDFs when there's no text layer to parse, and flags OCR-uncertain lines for a human glance.

Full Run History

Every reconciliation is saved with its totals and full line-item detail, so past runs are always one click away.

One-Click CSV Export

Export any run's complete item-level detail for records, follow-up, or your accounting team.

How it works

From two files to a reconciled report

1

Drop the files

Upload the carrier statement and the AMS360 export — PDF, Excel, or CSV.

2

Let it match

The engine lines up every policy by number, falling back to name and amount when it has to.

3

Review what's flagged

Mismatched amounts, missing policies, and OCR-uncertain lines are called out by name, not buried in a total.

4

Export and keep the record

Pull a CSV of the full run, and come back to any past reconciliation whenever you need it.
