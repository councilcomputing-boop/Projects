The problem

Spreadsheets weren't built for a bond desk

Bond registers grow fast, live across multiple files, and rarely agree with the carrier's numbers by the time reconciliation rolls around.

Scattered records

Bid bonds, final bonds, and license bonds tracked in different tabs or files, with no single view of a principal's full bond history.

Manual reconciliation

Matching your register against a carrier statement line-by-line, by hand, every single month — and hoping nothing gets missed.

No audit trail

When a bond amount or status changes, there's no record of who changed it, when, or what it looked like before.

What BondDesk does

Everything your bond desk needs

Purpose-built for surety bond tracking — not a repurposed spreadsheet or generic CRM.

Full Bond Lifecycle

Track Bid, Final, License, and Maintenance bonds from quote to closeout. Convert a winning bid straight into a final bond without re-keying a single field.

Automatic Reconciliation

Load carrier statement figures against your register and let BondDesk flag matches, discrepancies, and missing bonds automatically.

Complete Audit Trail

Every edit is logged — who made it, when, and what changed. Nothing gets altered quietly, and nothing gets lost.

One-Click Reporting

Export filtered bond registers to PDF or Excel for producers, underwriters, or leadership — formatted and ready to send.

Role-Based Access

Admins manage the team through secure email invites. Everyone else sees exactly what they need for their book of business.

Work-on-Hand Visibility

Track contractor capacity and bid results — won or lost — alongside every bond, so you always know where a principal stands.

How it works

From quote to closeout, in four steps

1

Log the bid

Enter the principal, obligee, project, and bid bond percentage the moment a bid goes out.

2

Award it

One click turns an awarded bid bond into a final bond, carrying over every detail already on file.

3

Reconcile monthly

Match your register against carrier statements and resolve discrepancies before they pile up.

4

Report with confidence

Pull an audit-ready PDF or Excel export whenever producers or leadership ask for one.
